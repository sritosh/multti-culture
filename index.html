What is Multti Culture

A convergence of subcultures.

Multti Culture is Atti Culture's culture convention — the day Chennai's scenes collide under one roof. Music, fashion, food, art and the communities that carry them, all in one building, all in one day. Not a single show but a whole world to move through.

Season 2 runs as three back-to-back sessions you can dip into or do the full run: a hands-on production masterclass in the afternoon, a live podcast taping in front of a real crowd, and a headline live music showcase to close the night.
