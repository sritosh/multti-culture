Studio Sessions

A 3-hour mixing & production masterclass — the philosophy behind the sound. Dive deep into the techniques that shape modern music.

About the Masterclass

Whether you are an aspiring producer or an experienced sound engineer, Studio Sessions is designed to break down the barriers between you and professional-grade audio.

Led by industry veterans, this session covers everything from room acoustics and mic placement to advanced EQ, compression, and spatial mixing techniques. We will tear down multi-tracks of released songs and show you exactly how the magic happens.

What you'll learn:

  • The philosophy of sound selection
  • Advanced dynamic processing
  • Building space with reverb and delay
  • Mixing for different playback systems
